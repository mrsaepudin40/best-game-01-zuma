HOW TO PLAY

  • Aim with the mouse — viewed from above, the widow crouches at the spiral's centre and its fangs follow your cursor. Click to launch an orb.
  • Match 3 or more orbs of the same colour to shatter them. Chains of 4+ build your combo multiplier.
  • Clear the whole chain before its head reaches the portal at the spiral's heart, or you lose a life.
  • Space swaps your loaded orb. 1–6 fire a stored power-up. P pauses, M mutes, F toggles the FPS meter.
  • Power orbs: Bomb blasts a radius, Lightning zaps eight orbs, Slow freezes the chain, Rainbow matches any colour, Laser cuts a line, Reverse pushes the chain back.
  • Boss levels (4, 8, 12) bring armored orbs (hit twice) and locked orbs (clear neighbours first). Earn coins, spend them in the shop between levels.
  • Finish all 12 levels to win the campaign, then keep going in Endless mode.
